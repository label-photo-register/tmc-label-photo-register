Label Photo Register V2.3 — QR-first OCR improvement

- QR Code now has priority for Label No., complete Lot No., Part No. and Net Weight (if the QR payload matches the expected format).
- Description and Spec are read separately from small printed text rows instead of a large combined block.
- Unclear OCR values are not silently accepted. Check the photo before saving.
- Existing Chinese/English/Malay interface, saved labels, photo grouping, Excel export, shareable XLSM conversion and safe export cleanup remain.
- Photo files and real company QR payloads are NOT included in this upload package.
- Test first on your Android phone using Label 009396 and other labels. QR recognition is browser-dependent; a JavaScript fallback is loaded from jsDelivr when connected.
- Existing saved records are not erased; open a saved record and click Recognize again before saving corrected details.
